How to play

Turn a layer: click a sticker and its face turns counter-clockwise (⟲); right-click turns it clockwise (⟳). Shift+click turns the middle slice next to it, Ctrl+click the whole cube. Or use the ⟳ and ⟲ buttons under the cube, or the keys: a letter turns clockwise, Shift+letter the other way.

Letters: U, D, L, R, F, B are the up, down, left, right, front and back faces, clockwise as you look at that face; ' (prime) means the other way, so R' turns R back. In "More": x, y, z turn the whole cube; M, E, S the middle slices.

Look around: drag anywhere on the cube or around it.

Undo (Ctrl+Z) takes back the last turn. Scramble mixes the cube up and starts the timer on your first turn; Reset puts it back solved.
